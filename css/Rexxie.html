Jollof Rice Recipe

Jollof Rice

Jollof rice is a delicious and flavorful West African dish made with rice, tomatoes, peppers, and spices. It is a popular and tasty meal that can be enjoyed with family and friends.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of rice
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 2 red peppers
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tablespoons of vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon of salt
  • 1 teaspoon of curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon of thyme
  • 2 cups of water

Instructions

  1. Wash the rice thoroughly and set it aside.
  2. Blend the tomatoes, peppers, and onion until smooth.
  3. Heat the vegetable oil in a pot and add the blended mixture.
  4. Cook the tomato mixture for about 10–15 minutes.
  5. Add salt, curry powder, and thyme.
  6. Add the washed rice and water, then stir everything together.
  7. Cover the pot and cook on low heat until the rice is soft.
  8. Serve hot and enjoy your delicious jollof rice!
